# IEEE 802.15.4 acceptance scenarios

Predecessor HIL catalog, state of 2026-10-16. Unless a scenario says
otherwise, it runs on channel 15 with PAN 0x4f45, device short address 0x0001,
peer 0x0002 and foreign address 0x0099.

The "vendor 802.15.4 peer" is an ESP32-C5 running an ESP-IDF application on
the vendor 802.15.4 driver, driven over a line protocol. It transmits at 0 dBm
unless the scenario sets another power. The "OpenThread peer" is an ESP32-C5
running the ESP-IDF OpenThread stack.

## Air check: scan, CCA, direct and scheduled TX/RX

- Checks: each cycle on the shared radio arbiter runs, in order:
  1. energy scan;
  2. CCA;
  3. direct transmit;
  4. two ETM-scheduled transmits;
  5. a promiscuous receive window;
  6. an ETM-scheduled receive window;
  7. stop.
- Gate:
  - 2 boots × 2 cycles; the stop is Complete with every cycle done.
  - Energy reading between −110 and +10 dBm. CCA may read Clear or Busy.
  - Every transmit ends Success, never before its start time.
  - Each scheduled transmit completes within 10 ms of its start; the two do
    not overlap.
  - Scheduled receive window (lead 20 ms, window 50 ms):
    - no frame before the window opens;
    - with no frame, it ends between its end time and +10 ms;
    - otherwise, it ends within 10 ms of its first frame.
  - Energy scan 5 ms; promiscuous receive window 500 ms.
- Peer: none.
- Hardware: fail. It never passed: a brownout reset on every predecessor run
  up to 2026-10-11.

## Shared-PHY maintenance beside a receiving MAC

- Checks: periodic shared-PHY tracking runs while the MAC receives, without
  any failure. A direct transmit afterwards still succeeds, and the stop ends
  Done.
- Gate: 2 boots, 10 tracking periods of 1 s (session 10.5 s), with at least
  ceil(periods / 2) = 5 ticks tracked.
- Peer: none.
- Hardware: pass, predecessor HIL run 2026-10-11.

## Channel energy and CCA against a peer burst

- Checks: the C5 sends a back-to-back burst of 125-byte frames. The test then
  compares the following (each level is a median of 4 samples, 2 ms energy
  scans):
  - burst energy against the baseline;
  - a far channel (25) during the burst;
  - retuning back to the burst channel;
  - burst energy against the RSSI of the burst frames;
  - the level after the burst;
  - CCA results and channel-assessed transmissions.
- Gate:
  - Burst at least 15 dB above the baseline, and at least 15 dB above the far
    channel.
  - Retuned level within 6 dB of the burst level. The level after the burst is
    at most 6 dB above the baseline.
  - Burst energy within 10 dB of the frame RSSI.
  - At least half of the burst CCAs read busy, and more than in the baseline.
  - At least half of the assessed transmissions end ChannelBusy.
  - At least one burst frame is received.
  - 2 boots; 50 ms settle after the burst starts.
- Peer: ESP32-C5 vendor 802.15.4 peer.
- Hardware: pass, predecessor HIL run 2026-10-11.

## Live RSSI follows the last frame and the peer's power

- Checks: the live recent-RSSI read agrees with the RSSI of the last received
  frame. It follows a step in peer transmit power from +20 to −15 dBm.
- Gate:
  - Live read within 3 dB of the frame RSSI in both series.
  - Strong series at least 10 dB above the weak series.
  - 8 frames per series (without ACK request), at least 1 received per
    series; 2 boots.
- Peer: ESP32-C5 vendor 802.15.4 peer.
- Hardware: pass, predecessor HIL run 2026-09-29. On 2026-09-27 the live read
  was within 2 dB of the frame RSSI and followed the 36 dB step.

## Data exchange with a vendor peer: ACK, filtering, frame pending, enhanced ACK

- Checks:
  - **Device to peer:** CSMA-CA data frames (at most 4 backoffs, 3 retries)
    are acknowledged with the matching sequence number and no frame pending,
    and arrive intact.
  - **Peer to device:** frames are acknowledged by the device and reported
    with the same CRC32C and length. The count is exact.
  - **Filtering:** a device frame to a foreign address ends
    NoAcknowledgement. A peer frame to a foreign address is neither
    acknowledged nor reported.
  - **Frame pending:** with the peer in the pending table, the ACK to its data
    request carries frame pending.
  - **Enhanced ACK:** a 2015 frame receives a byte-exact IEEE 802.15.4-2015
    enhanced ACK with pending set.
  - **After 1.2 s:** due PHY maintenance reports Tracked (within 10 s), then
    one acknowledged frame goes each way.
- Gate: 2 boots × 8 frames per direction, every frame successful. Counts and
  bytes are exact; there is no success ratio.
- Peer: ESP32-C5 vendor 802.15.4 peer.
- Hardware: pass, predecessor HIL run 2026-10-11. Earlier runs failed on
  brownout or a broken peer console.

## Data exchange with Wi-Fi coexistence enabled

- Checks: the vendor-peer exchange above with Wi-Fi coexistence requested.
  Coexistence reports enabled, and leaving it does not fail.
- Gate: as above.
- Peer: ESP32-C5 vendor 802.15.4 peer.
- Hardware: pass, predecessor HIL run 2026-10-11.

## Thread child attach and UDP exchange

- Checks:
  - The peer forms a Thread network and becomes leader within 20 s.
  - The device attaches as a Child with a mesh-local EID within 60 s.
  - UDP datagrams on port 1212 arrive intact in both directions, in order,
    each from the expected EID, within 5 s each.
  - The stop ends Done.
- Gate: 1 boot, 3 datagrams per direction.
- Peer: ESP32-C5 OpenThread peer (leader).
- Hardware: pass, predecessor HIL run 2026-10-11.

## Shared-PHY tracking hold on an idle 802.15.4 receiver

- Checks: forced shared-PHY tracking ticks (RFPLL, calibration, both) are
  timed on the device while 802.15.4 receives on an idle radio.
  - Periodic tracking suspends and resumes.
  - No tick is Failed, Skipped or invalid, and every configuration times at
    least one tick.
- Gate: 2 boots, 3 ticks per configuration. Hold durations are recorded, not
  bounded.
- Peer: none.
- Hardware: pass, predecessor HIL run 2026-10-11. Measured: common bracket
  about 6.4 ms, TX about 7.2 ms, tick about 14 ms.

## Polled ED transactions and a selective event write (RF closed)

- Checks:
  - Two back-to-back polled energy-detect transactions.
  - Then a selective write of ED-DONE and TIMER0, with the interrupt route
    detached. Each write clears only its own bit.
  - The event enable is masked before and after, with only ED-DONE, TIMER0
    and RX-abort enabled while active.
  - The RX-abort enable is restored, there is no timeout STOP, and cleanup
    ends Clear.
- Gate:
  - Each ED completes within 1..100 000 polls; ED duration 8.
  - TIMER0 shows bounded activity (threshold 1000).
  - 3 boots.
  - The checkpoint states are otherwise exact equalities.
- Peer: none.
- Hardware: pass (flaky), predecessor HIL run 2026-10-11. It failed on every
  run for a period until a fix, and 4 of 10 runs failed overall.

## Selective timer event acknowledgement

- Checks: the two enabled timer bits are acknowledged selectively, and a later
  distinct TIMER1 arrival is observed, with the route detached. Ten exact
  event-state checkpoints run from reset Clear to final Clear. Cleanup is Clear
  or TIMER1 only.
- Gate: 3 boots, poll limit 100 000, timer threshold 1000. Both timers show
  bounded activity.
- Peer: none.
- Hardware: pass, predecessor HIL run 2026-10-11.

## Interrupt route probe: same-bit coalescing and level retrigger (RF closed)

- Checks:
  - The stop is Complete. The polled snapshot and its control both read TIMER0
    only.
  - The same-bit outcome is Coalesced or Retained, recorded and identical on
    every boot.
  - Level retrigger enters exactly twice: first TIMER0 with TIMER0+TIMER1
    pending, then TIMER1 only.
  - The final events are Clear.
- Gate: 3 boots; timer threshold 100 µs; settle 2 ms.
- Peer: none.
- Hardware: pass, predecessor HIL run 2026-10-11.
